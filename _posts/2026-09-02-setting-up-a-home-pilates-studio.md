Every few months a client asks me the same question, usually after a good session: "Should I just get a Reformer for the house?"

Sometimes the answer is yes. Often it is "not yet." Here is how I think about it.

## Start with the room, not the machine

A Reformer needs roughly 3 feet by 9 feet of clear floor, plus space to stand at the foot end and walk around it. Add a Tower and you need ceiling height and a wall you can anchor to. Most clients have this space; fewer have it somewhere they will actually use it. A machine in a spare bedroom you never enter is furniture.

The best home studios I have seen are in a room with a window, a hard floor or low pile rug, and nothing else in it. Light and quiet do more for a practice than any accessory.

## Which Reformer

I teach on Balanced Body and I recommend it for the home, for the same reasons: the spring gauges are consistent, the carriage runs true for decades, and parts are available. Two models cover almost everyone.

- **The Allegro 2** is the right answer for most homes. It is a full-size, full-spring Reformer that stows upright when you need the room back. With the Tower it becomes a complete apparatus.
- **The Studio Reformer** is the maple-frame machine you see in the studio. It is beautiful, heavier, and permanent. Choose it if the room is dedicated and you want the piece to be part of the house.

Skip the compact "Pilates machines" sold on television. They are not Reformers and the spring loads will not carry a programme.

## What to buy alongside it

Three things, all inexpensive next to the machine: a good mat, a Magic Circle, and a set of weighted balls. With those and a Reformer, I can teach almost the entire repertoire in your home.

## What matters more than the machine

Instruction. A Reformer with a poor programme reinforces every habit you already have, only now with springs. If you buy one, plan for private sessions on it, at least at the start, so the machine is set up for your body and you know what to do on the days I am not there. That is exactly what the in-home programme is for, and I am glad to help you choose and order the right equipment before it arrives.
