I have been to retreats that were a group class schedule with a nicer view. That is not what I want to make.

A private session works because it is built around one body over time. A retreat compresses that time. Four days, two sessions a day, the same instructor watching the same eight people, is the equivalent of two months of weekly work. Habits change faster when there is nowhere else to be.

<!--more-->

## What it will be

Small. Eight guests at most, so every Reformer session is a duet and every mat class is taught, not led. Daily apparatus work, a sunrise mat session, breathwork, and one form of bodywork each day, with real food cooked by someone whose only job that week is to feed you well. The house will be private, within a few hours of Las Vegas, chosen for quiet and light.

## What it will not be

Crowded, loud, or a sales event. There will be no merchandise, no ambassador programme, and no morning where you are asked to film content.

## Why the waitlist comes first

I want to plan the first one around the people who are coming. Dates, the house, and the bodywork on offer will be decided with the waitlist, and the waitlist will hear first. If that sounds like you, add your name on the home page. There is no deposit and no obligation.
